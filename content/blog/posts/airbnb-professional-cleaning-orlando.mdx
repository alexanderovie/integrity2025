# How Professional Cleaning Boosts Your Airbnb Ratings in Orlando

Orlando is one of the world's most popular tourist destinations, and the Airbnb market is highly competitive. The difference between a successful host and one who struggles can be as simple as cleaning quality.

- **4.8+ stars** in cleaning = 40% more bookings
- **5-star reviews** = $50-100 more per night on average
- **1 point less** in rating = 10-15% less income

## Cleaning Standards for Airbnb

### What Guests Expect (and Check)

**Critical areas:**

1. **Beds:** Clean sheets, no hair, no odors
2. **Bathrooms:** No mold, no hair, no water stains
3. **Kitchen:** Clean appliances, no food remnants
4. **Floors:** No dust, no pet hair, no stains
5. **Windows:** Clean and streak-free
6. **Furniture:** No dust, no stains, organized

**Details that make the difference:**

- Clean blinds (both sides)
- Dust-free vents
- Streak-free mirrors
- Shiny faucets
- Fresh odors (not just masked)
- Areas behind furniture

## The Problem with "Homemade" Cleaning

### Common Host Mistakes

**1. Quick Cleaning**
- Quick wipe-down isn't enough
- Guests notice details
- Hidden areas accumulate dirt

**2. Inadequate Products**
- Products that leave residues
- Strong odors that bother
- Don't disinfect properly

**3. Lack of Consistency**
- Different standards each time
- Dependence on personal availability
- Fatigue that affects quality
