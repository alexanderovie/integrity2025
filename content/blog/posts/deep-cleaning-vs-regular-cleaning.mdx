# Deep Cleaning vs Regular Cleaning: Which One Do You Need?

When it comes to keeping your space clean, it's important to understand the differences between regular cleaning and deep cleaning. Each has its purpose and ideal timing.

## What is Regular Cleaning?

Regular cleaning is a maintenance service performed periodically (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly) to keep your space in good condition.

### Includes:

- Vacuuming and mopping floors
- Cleaning surfaces and furniture
- Bathroom cleaning (toilets, sinks, showers)
- Basic kitchen cleaning
- Emptying trash bins
- Basic organization

### Ideal for:

- Busy families who need constant maintenance
- Offices that require a professional daily environment
- Rental properties with tenants
- Spaces that are already in good condition and only need maintenance

## What is Deep Cleaning?

Deep cleaning is a comprehensive service that focuses on areas that are normally not cleaned in a regular cleaning.

### Includes everything from regular cleaning, plus:

- **Behind and under furniture and appliances**
- **Window cleaning (interior and exterior)**
- **Blinds and curtain cleaning**
- **Baseboards and moldings**
- **Vent and filter cleaning**
- **Deep oven and microwave cleaning**
- **Complete bathroom disinfection**
- **Cabinet cleaning (interior and exterior)**
- **Light fixtures and ceiling fan cleaning**
- **Dust removal in high areas**

### Ideal for:

- **Moves (move-in or move-out)**
- **After renovation or construction**
- **Airbnb properties that need to be spotless**
- **First time hiring professional service**
- **Before or after special events**
- **Seasonal changes (spring/fall)**

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | Regular Cleaning | Deep Cleaning |
|---------|------------------|---------------|
| Frequency | Weekly/Bi-weekly/Monthly | As needed |
| Duration | 2-4 hours | 4-8 hours |
| Focus | Maintenance | Complete transformation |
| Hidden areas | No | Yes |
| Disinfection | Basic | Complete |
| Ideal for | Maintenance | Special situations |

## When Do You Need Each One?

### Choose Regular Cleaning if:

- Your space is already in good condition
- You need constant maintenance
- You have a fixed monthly budget
- You want to maintain a high standard without effort

### Choose Deep Cleaning if:

- It's your first time hiring professional cleaning
- You're moving (in or out)
- You haven't had professional cleaning in over 6 months
- You have an important event coming up
- You need your property to pass an inspection
