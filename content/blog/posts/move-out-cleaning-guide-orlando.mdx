# Complete Move-Out Cleaning Guide for Orlando: Recover Your Security Deposit

Moving can be stressful, but losing your security deposit due to insufficient cleaning can be devastating. This guide will help ensure you recover every penny of your deposit.

## Why Move-Out Cleaning is Critical

Landlords in Orlando can withhold part or all of your deposit if the property isn't in the same condition as when you received it. Professional cleaning can make the difference between recovering $1,500 or losing it.

## Move-Out Cleaning Checklist

### Kitchen

**Appliances:**
- [ ] Refrigerator: Interior and exterior, including shelves, drawers, and gaskets
- [ ] Oven: Complete interior, including racks and trays
- [ ] Microwave: Interior and exterior
- [ ] Dishwasher: Interior and filters
- [ ] Stove: Burners, grates, and area underneath

**Surfaces:**
- [ ] Cabinets: Interior and exterior
- [ ] Countertops: Deep cleaning and disinfection
- [ ] Sink: Descaling and faucet polishing
- [ ] Ventilation: Range hood cleaning

### Bathrooms

**Deep cleaning:**
- [ ] Shower/Tub: Mold and water stain removal
- [ ] Toilet: Interior, exterior, and area behind
- [ ] Sink: Descaling and polishing
- [ ] Mirrors: No streaks or stains
- [ ] Tiles: Grout cleaning
- [ ] Cabinets: Interior and exterior

### Bedrooms and Common Areas

**Complete cleaning:**
- [ ] Closets: Empty and clean
- [ ] Windows: Interior and frames
- [ ] Blinds: Both sides cleaned
- [ ] Baseboards: Complete cleaning
- [ ] Vents: Grille cleaning
- [ ] Doors: Frame and handle cleaning
- [ ] Light switches: Plate cleaning

### Floors

**By type:**
- [ ] Carpets: Deep vacuuming and stain removal
- [ ] Hard floors: Mopping and drying
- [ ] Baseboards: Complete cleaning

### Outdoor Areas (if applicable)

- [ ] Patio/Balcony: Cleaning and sweeping
- [ ] Garage: Cleaning and organization
- [ ] Storage: Areas empty and clean

## Common Mistakes That Cause Deposit Loss

### 1. Superficial Cleaning

**Mistake:** Only cleaning what's visible
**Solution:** Deep cleaning of hidden areas (behind appliances, inside cabinets)

### 2. Ignoring Minor Damage

**Mistake:** Leaving small damage unrepaired
**Solution:** Repair nail holes, minor scratches, etc.

### 3. Not Documenting Initial Condition

**Mistake:** No photos of original condition
**Solution:** Take detailed photos when moving in and when cleaning

### 4. Using Incorrect Products

**Mistake:** Products that damage surfaces
**Solution:** Use appropriate products for each surface
